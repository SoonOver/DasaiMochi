# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **Dasai Mochi** community a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background,
identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus on technical merit and helping users across Linux and Windows.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers.
